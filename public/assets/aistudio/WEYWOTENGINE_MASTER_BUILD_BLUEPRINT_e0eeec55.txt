## 3. MATHEMATICAL ENGINE SPECIFICATION

### 3.1 Scale-Normalized Diagonal SPD Distance + Student-$t$ Kernel
1. Standardize log-transformed positive features and linear indices against Tier-1 catalog median and scaled IQR ($\mu_i^{\text{ref}}, \sigma_i^{\text{ref}} = \max(10^{-4}, 0.7413 \cdot \text{IQR}_i)$).
2. Apply dimensionless asymmetric breach penalties $\Psi_i(\Delta z_{k,i})$ directly to component residuals (`clayIndex > 0.05` on anhydrous lunar targets $\lambda=3.0$; `pPrecipGt5Mm > 0.15` on hyper-arid missions $\lambda=2.5$; `visualAlienation < 0.4` $\lambda=2.5$).
3. Over the observed non-null feature set $\mathcal{O}_k$ in domain $k$, compute the strictly positive weighted distance:
   $$d_k^2(\tau, \alpha, m) = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{O}_k} w_{k,i} \left(\Psi_i(\Delta z_{k,i})\right)^2, \qquad p_{\text{eff}, k} = \max\left(10^{-6}, \sum_{i \in \mathcal{O}_k} w_{k,i}\right)$$
4. Map via the **heavy-tailed Student-$t$ / Rational-Quadratic kernel ($\nu = 2.0$)**:
   $$S_k(\tau, \alpha, m) = 100 \cdot \left(1 + \frac{d_k^2(\tau, \alpha, m)}{2.0 \cdot p_{\text{eff}, k}}\right)^{-1.0}$$
   Also compute `domainFrontierPct` $= 100 \cdot S_k / \max_{\alpha'} S_k$ and composite `S_month(tau, alpha, m) = sum(w_k * S_k) / sum(w_k)`.

### 3.2 Ephemeris, 12-Month Interpolated Campaign Window & Deterministic Sensitivity
- **Mars $L_s$:** Solve $E - 0.0934\sin E = M(t_{\text{epoch}})$, get $\nu_{\text{rad}}$, convert to degrees: $L_s = (\frac{180}{\pi}\nu_{\text{rad}} + 250.99^\circ) \pmod{360^\circ}$. Modulate target temperature, $\text{CO}_2$ frost, and dust opacity.
- **Lunar $\beta_\odot$ & Horizon Mask:** Test solar elevation against target `horizonMaskMaxDeg` to distinguish sunlit ridges from cryogenic PSRs.
- **Continuous `[3, 90]`-Day Window Integration:** Given campaign start day-of-year $d_0 \in \{1..365\}$ and duration $\Delta t$, sample daily interpolated scores $S_{\text{day}}(d)$ across $d \in [d_0, d_0 + \Delta t - 1]$ from the 12 monthly bins:
  $$\bar{S}_{\text{win}} = \frac{1}{\Delta t}\sum_{d} S_{\text{day}}(d), \qquad \sigma_{\text{down}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{\Delta t}\sum_{d} \max(0, \bar{S}_{\text{win}} - S_{\text{day}}(d))^2}$$
  $$P_{\text{drift}} = \sigma_{\text{down}} \cdot \left(1 - e^{-\Delta t / 14.0}\right), \qquad P_{\text{rain}} = 1 - \prod_{d} (1 - P_{\text{wet}, >5\text{mm}}(d))^{1 / 3.0}$$
  $$\Omega_{\text{cliff}} = 20.0 \cdot \max\left(0, \frac{P_{\text{rain}} - 0.10}{0.90}\right) \cdot w_{\text{hydro}}, \qquad S_{\text{final}} = \max_{d_0} \max(0, \bar{S}_{\text{win}} - P_{\text{drift}} - \Omega_{\text{cliff}})$$
- **Deterministic Sensitivity Check:** For the Top 5 ranked candidates, evaluate $\pm 15\%$ domain weight perturbations deterministically to output `sensitivityRange: [minScore, maxScore]` and `top3RobustnessPct` (`0–100%`).

### 3.3 Multi-Target Matrix, Sparing AI & Non-Negative 2-Site Split Optimizer
- **Harmonic Versatility Index:** $\mathcal{V}(\alpha_j) = K / \sum_{i=1}^K \max(M_{i,j}, 10^{-3})^{-1}$.
- **Mean Pairwise Rank Dissonance:** $\mathcal{D}_{\text{rank}} = \frac{1}{\binom{K}{2}} \sum_{a<b} (1 - \rho_s(\mathbf{M}_{a,:}, \mathbf{M}_{b,:})) / 2$.
- **2-Site Non-Negative Portfolio Optimizer:** Solves $\max_{(\alpha_A, \alpha_B),\, \mathbf{w}_A + \mathbf{w}_B = \mathbf{w}_\mu,\; \mathbf{w}_A, \mathbf{w}_B \ge 0} \min(S_{\text{final}}(\tau, \alpha_A \mid \mathbf{w}_A),\, S_{\text{final}}(\tau, \alpha_B \mid \mathbf{w}_B))$ across binary/simplex 5D partitions—always accessible in the Portfolio tab and auto-triggered when $\max_\alpha S_{\text{final}} < 72\%$.
- **Sparing AI Brief Parser (`/api/v1/ai/parse-brief`):** Requires `targetIds` + `briefText`. Uses a deterministic ontology keyword/rule mapper (+ optional `temperature = 0` structured extraction) returning matched evidence chips, deterministic $C_{\text{conflict}}$, and **at most 1** multiple-choice clarifying question iff $C_{\text{conflict}} > 0.70$.
